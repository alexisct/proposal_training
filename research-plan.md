# Research Plan: Academic Funding Proposals and UKRI NERC Pushing the Frontiers

## Task Checklist

- [ ] Research what makes a strong academic funding proposal in general
- [ ] Research the current UKRI NERC Pushing the Frontiers call guidance and requirements
- [ ] Separate universal proposal-writing principles from call-specific expectations
- [ ] Identify likely reviewer priorities and evaluation questions
- [ ] Compare general best practice against NERC call requirements
- [ ] Summarize common weaknesses and risks to avoid
- [ ] Present findings in a practical, draft-ready format with citations

## Objective

Research what makes a good academic funding proposal in two layers:

1. General best practice for strong academic funding proposals
2. Specific expectations for the UKRI NERC Pushing the Frontiers call

The goal is to produce guidance that is both accurate and immediately useful for drafting, reviewing, or strengthening a proposal.

## Research Approach

### 1. General Proposal-Quality Research

I will identify the recurring features of strong academic funding proposals across reputable guidance sources. This will focus on factors such as:

- clarity of the research question
- significance and originality
- strength and fit of the methodology
- feasibility and deliverability
- credibility of the team
- alignment between aims, methods, and resources
- impact and broader value
- clarity of writing and structure

I will distinguish between:

- universal best practices that apply across most academic funders
- advice that is discipline-specific or funder-specific

### 2. UKRI NERC Pushing the Frontiers Call Research

I will review the current official call materials and related guidance to extract the specific expectations for this scheme, including:

- call aims and scope
- eligibility requirements
- assessment criteria
- application structure
- budget and costing rules
- timelines and submission requirements
- any call-specific signals about what reviewers are likely to value

### 3. Gap Analysis

I will compare general “good proposal” guidance against the specific NERC call requirements to identify:

- where standard good practice is sufficient
- where the call requires a different emphasis
- where proposals may fail by being strong in general but weak in funder fit

### 4. Reviewer-Minded Interpretation

I will translate formal criteria into practical reviewer-style questions, such as:

- Why this project now?
- Why this team?
- Why this approach?
- Why is this frontier research rather than incremental work?
- Why is this proposal credible within the time, budget, and risk profile?

### 5. Practical Writing Framework

I will turn the research into a usable framework rather than just a summary. This will likely include:

- the top success factors
- common weaknesses to avoid
- a call-specific checklist
- a suggested structure for drafting or reviewing the proposal

## Sources I Plan to Prioritize

- official UKRI and NERC call documents
- official application guidance and assessment criteria
- reputable university research office guidance
- trusted academic proposal-writing resources

I will keep primary-source requirements separate from my interpretation and cite sources clearly.

## How Findings Will Be Presented

### Executive Summary

A concise overview of what makes a strong proposal overall and what matters most for this specific NERC call.

### General Guidance Section

A distilled summary of best practices for academic funding proposals, grouped into clear themes.

### NERC-Specific Section

A focused explanation of what this call appears to reward, including constraints, priorities, and nuances.

### Comparison Table

A side-by-side comparison of:

- general good proposal practice
- NERC Pushing the Frontiers expectations
- implications for this application

### Practical Checklist

A concise checklist that can be used to assess a draft proposal.

### Optional Follow-On Outputs

If useful after the research, this can be turned into:

- a proposal outline
- a reviewer-style critique framework
- a scoring rubric
- draft-specific editing guidance
